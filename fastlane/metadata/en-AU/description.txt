Integra Markets reads the commodity news flow and tells you what moved, and why.

WHY IT IS DIFFERENT

Most sentiment tools hand you a number. A number you cannot check is an opinion with a decimal point.

Every reading here carries its evidence: the named signal that fired, and the exact phrase from the article that triggered it. "Price action down - Gold slips" takes two seconds to verify by eye. That is the whole design.

WHAT IT COVERS

Energy: crude, refined products, natural gas, LPG, thermal and coking coal
Metals: iron ore, copper, gold, silver, lithium, uranium
Agriculture: wheat, corn, soybeans, sugar, coffee, cocoa, fertiliser
Also: freight, FX and weather, where a shipping disruption or a currency move becomes a supply story

ONE METHOD, EVERY MARKET

- Current sentiment per commodity, with the drivers behind it
- Day-on-day momentum, so you see a change rather than a level
- The headlines that set the reading, summarised
- Alerts on the commodities you follow

BUILT ON A REAL ARCHIVE

339,000 scored documents going back to 2020, with named evidence on more than 52,000 of them. Sentiment history is computed from that record, not inferred from today's snapshot.

FOR DESK USE

A read-only HTTP API and a Claude connector are available on paid plans, so the same data reaches your notebook, your spreadsheet or your model. No SDK to install.

ONE HONEST LIMIT

Named signals fire more often in recent coverage than in the deep archive - roughly 30% of recent articles against 15% further back. If you are calibrating against history, that skew matters, and you should hear it from us rather than discover it.

Integra Markets is an information service. It is not investment advice, and nothing in it is a recommendation to trade.
